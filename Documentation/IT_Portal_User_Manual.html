1. Getting Started

1.1 Logging In

1

Open the IT Portal

Open your web browser (Chrome or Edge recommended) and navigate to the IT Portal URL provided by your IT department.

2

Enter Your Credentials

Type your email address and password on the login page. Click "Login".

3

First-Time Password Change

If this is your first login or your password was recently reset, you will be redirected to the Change Password page. Your new password must be at least 10 characters and include uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a special character.

⚠️ Account Lockout
After 5 failed password attempts, your account will be temporarily locked. Wait a few minutes and try again, or contact IT for assistance.

2. Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first page you see after login. It provides an overview of your pending tasks and system statistics tailored to your role.

For HR Users

  • Pending Queues — Lists of onboarding/offboarding requests awaiting employee signatures or completion.
  • Monthly Trends — Charts showing onboarding vs. offboarding activity over the past 12 months.
  • Completed This Month — Count of requests completed in the current month.

For IT Admin Users

  • Unified Task Queue — All pending onboarding and offboarding requests in one list, sorted by urgency.
  • Overdue Alerts — Requests that have exceeded their SLA target days are highlighted in red.
  • System Health — Active users count, total audit log entries, total assets, total licenses.
  • Expiring Soon — Assets with warranties or licenses expiring within 30 days.

For Managers

  • Pending Approvals — Offboarding requests awaiting your HOD sign-off.
✅ Quick Action
Click on any item in the pending queue to go directly to that request's detail page.

3. Onboarding — New Employee Setup

Use this module when a new employee joins the company and needs IT resources (accounts, laptop, software, email groups, etc.).

👤 Who Uses This?
HR creates the request → IT completes the setup → Employee signs acknowledgement.

3.1 Creating a New Onboarding Request HR

Step 1 — Employee Details

1

Go to Onboarding

Click "Onboarding" in the sidebar, then click "+ New Onboarding" button.

2

Fill in Employee Information

Enter the following required details:

  • Full Name — Employee's full name
  • Employee ID — Company employee number
  • Designation — Job title (e.g., "Accounts Executive")
  • Company/Branch — Select from dropdown
  • Department — Select from dropdown (filtered by selected company)
  • Join Date — The employee's first day
  • Email Address — The new company email for this employee
  • Contact Number — Phone number
  • HOD Email — Head of Department's email for notifications
3

Click "Next"

The form is saved as a Draft and you proceed to Step 2.

Step 2 — Software & Access Requirements

4

Select Required Software

Check the boxes for software the employee needs (e.g., MS Office, Antivirus, AutoCAD). Mandatory software is pre-selected.

5

Select Email Groups & Shared Mailboxes

Choose the distribution groups and shared mailboxes the employee should be added to.

6

Submit to IT

Click "Submit". The request status changes to Pending IT Approval and the IT team is notified.

3.2 Processing the Request IT

Step 3 — Asset & Software Assignment

7

Assign Hardware

In the "Issued Assets" section, click "+ Add Asset". Search for available assets by asset tag. Select the asset type (Laptop, Monitor, etc.) and link it to the request.

8

Assign Software Licenses

In the "Issued Software" section, select the specific license edition to assign to this employee.

9

Set Folder Permissions

Tick the server folders and set Read and/or Modify permissions for each.

Step 4 — IT Setup Checklist & Signing

10

Complete IT Checklist

Check off each setup task as completed:

  • ☐ Physical setup & cleaning
  • ☐ O365 account setup
  • ☐ Windows login created
  • ☐ Email configured
  • ☐ Software installed
  • ☐ Domain joined
  • ☐ WiFi, Printer, VPN configured
11

Sign & Send for Employee Acknowledgement

Draw your digital signature on the signature pad, then click "Sign & Submit". An email with a secure signing link is automatically sent to the employee.

3.3 Employee Acknowledgement Employee

12

Open the Signing Link

The employee receives an email with a one-time signing link. No login is required — simply click the link in the email.

13

Review & Sign

Review the list of assigned assets and software. Tick the PDPA consent checkbox, draw a signature, and click "Submit". The request is now Completed.

3.4 Other Actions

ActionHow
View request detailsClick the request row in the list, or click the "View" button
Edit a draft requestOpen the request and click "Edit" (only available for Draft status)
Reject a requestIT can click "Reject" and provide a reason
Download PDFOn the Details page, click "Download PDF" to get a printable copy
Delete a requestClick "Delete" (confirmation required)

4. Offboarding — Employee Exit

Use this module when an employee is leaving the company. It ensures all IT resources are properly returned and accounts are deactivated.

👤 Approval Flow
HR creates → HOD approves → IT processes → Employee signs.

4.1 Creating an Offboarding Request HR

1

Start New Offboarding

Go to Offboarding → Click "+ New Offboarding".

2

Fill in Employee Details

Enter the departing employee's information. You can search by name to auto-fill from existing records. Set the Last Day of Work and select the HOD for approval.

3

Upload Resignation Letter (Optional)

Attach the resignation letter document if available.

4

Sign & Submit

Draw your HR signature and submit. The request goes to Pending HOD Approval.

4.2 HOD Approval Manager

5

Review & Approve

The assigned HOD receives an email notification. Open the request from your Dashboard or the Offboarding list. Review the details, draw your signature, and click "Approve". The request moves to Pending IT Approval.

4.3 IT Processing IT

6

Complete IT Checklist

Open the request and work through the offboarding checklist:

  • ☐ Active Directory account disabled
  • ☐ Email forwarding set up
  • ☐ M365 license revoked
  • ☐ Software licenses recovered
7

Hardware Return & Clearance

Record returned items (laptop, accessories, keys). If items are damaged, upload photos and add notes. Assess fines for missing items if applicable.

8

Sign & Send to Employee

Draw your IT signature. An acknowledgement link is sent to the employee's email.

4.4 Follow-Up Actions IT

After the employee signs, complete the follow-up on the Details page:

  • ☐ Data backup completed
  • ☐ Email backup completed
  • ☐ Email forwarding configured (specify recipient)
  • ☐ All licenses removed

6. IT Asset Management IT Only

Track all company hardware — from purchase through assignment to disposal.

6.1 Viewing Assets

Go to IT Assets in the sidebar. You'll see a searchable, sortable table of all hardware. Use the filters at the top to narrow by status, type, or company.

6.2 Adding a New Asset

1

Click "+ Add Asset". Fill in: Asset Tag (unique), Serial Number, Asset Type, Model, Company, Location, Purchase Date, Warranty Expiry, and hardware specs (RAM, Storage, CPU). Click "Save".

6.3 Asset Statuses

StatusMeaning
AvailableReady to be assigned to a new employee
IssuedCurrently assigned to an employee via an onboarding request
Under RepairSent for maintenance — not available for assignment
DisposedEnd-of-life — permanently removed from inventory
LostCannot be located — flagged for investigation

6.4 Maintenance Logs

On each asset's detail page, you can record maintenance activity:

  • Service date, description of work, service provider, cost, and notes.
  • All maintenance history is preserved for audit and reporting purposes.

6.5 Warranty Monitoring

The system automatically checks for upcoming warranty expirations and sends email alerts to IT. Assets expiring within 30 days are flagged on the Dashboard.

7. Software & License Management IT Only

7.1 Software Catalog (Standard Software)

The "Software List" page shows all software types your organization uses. Each software can have multiple license editions/versions.

Adding a New Software

1

Click "+ Add Software". Enter the software name and mark it as Mandatory (auto-selected during onboarding) and/or Free (no license tracking needed).

7.2 License Editions

Under each software, you can add specific license editions (e.g., "Microsoft Office 2021 Pro", "Microsoft Office 365 E3"). Each edition tracks:

  • Contract Limit — How many licenses you purchased
  • Vendor Name and support contact
  • Expiry Date — For subscription-based licenses

7.3 License Keys

Within each license edition, you can store individual license keys. Keys are encrypted at rest for security. Each key shows whether it's assigned and to whom.

✅ Utilization Tracking
The Dashboard shows license utilization — how many keys are assigned vs. total available — helping you plan purchases.

9. Digital Signatures

The portal uses digital signature pads to capture legally-binding signatures at each approval stage.

9.1 How to Sign

1

Locate the Signature Pad

On the relevant step or detail page, scroll down to the Signature section.

2

Draw Your Signature

Use your mouse or finger (on touch devices) to draw your signature on the canvas. Click "Clear" to redo.

3

Submit

Click the "Sign & Submit" button. Your signature is saved with a timestamp and integrity code (HMAC).

🔒 Security Note
All signatures are protected with HMAC integrity verification. The system can detect if a signature has been tampered with. Signatures are embedded in the generated PDF documents.

9.2 Signature Flow per Module

Offboarding① HR → ② HOD → ③ IT Admin → ④ Employee

11. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email address. You will receive an OTP (One-Time Password) via email. Enter the OTP and set a new password.

Q: How do I know if a request is overdue?

Overdue requests are highlighted in red on the Dashboard and in the request list. The system also sends automatic email alerts when SLA deadlines are breached.

Q: Can I edit a request after submitting it?

You can only edit requests in Draft status. Once submitted, the request follows the approval workflow. Contact IT if changes are needed.

Q: The employee signing link expired. What do I do?

IT can resend the signing link from the request's detail page by clicking "Resend Signing Link".

Q: How do I download a PDF copy of a completed request?

Open the request's Details page and click the "Download PDF" button. The PDF includes all form data, signatures, and timestamps.

Q: I'm an HR user but I can't see the Onboarding menu?

Your account may not have the HR role assigned. Contact your IT administrator to verify your role under System Users.

Q: What is PDPA consent?

PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act) consent is a checkbox the employee must tick during signing to acknowledge that their personal data is collected and processed for IT management purposes.

Q: How do I bulk-import contacts into the Email Directory?

IT Go to Email Directory → Click "Import" → Upload an Excel file with columns: Name, Email, Department. The system will add all contacts and skip duplicates.

12. Glossary

TermDefinition
OnboardingThe process of setting up IT resources for a new employee joining the company.
OffboardingThe process of deactivating IT resources and collecting equipment when an employee leaves.
HODHead of Department — the manager responsible for approving offboarding requests.
SLAService Level Agreement — the target number of days to complete a request. Exceeding this triggers alerts.
Asset TagA unique identifier label attached to each piece of IT hardware.
PDPAPersonal Data Protection Act — a legal framework governing the collection and use of personal data.
HMACHash-based Message Authentication Code — used to verify digital signatures haven't been tampered with.
OTPOne-Time Password — a temporary code sent via email for password reset verification.
DraftA request that has been started but not yet submitted for approval.
Soft DeleteRecords are never permanently removed. Deleted items are hidden but preserved for audit purposes.
Audit LogA chronological record of every action performed in the system, including who did what and when.
